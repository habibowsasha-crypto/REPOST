TgBlaster v1.0.22b28 — @SpamBot repeated /start loop fix
=========================================================

Built strictly from v1.0.22b27.

Fixed:
1. A repeated PeerFlood no longer resets an already scheduled @SpamBot check.
2. After @SpamBot confirms the account is free, the same restriction episode
   cannot continuously reopen /start checks before one first DM is confirmed sent.
3. A new restriction cycle is automatically re-armed after a confirmed sent first DM.
4. Confirmed-send detection checks both legacy dm_pending_queue and unified
   dm_unified_leads rows and never treats timeout/uncertain pacing timestamps as success.

Not changed:
- first-DM texts, templates, selection, filters, delays and limits;
- automatic resume behavior after a valid free @SpamBot reply;
- Kirill VIP and AI funnel behavior;
- unified queue safety logic introduced in v1.0.22b27.

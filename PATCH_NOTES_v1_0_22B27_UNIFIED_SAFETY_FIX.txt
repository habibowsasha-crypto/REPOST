TgBlaster v1.0.22b27 — unified queue safety and reliability fix
===============================================================

Built strictly from v1.0.22b26.

Fixed without adding new recipient limits, bans, filters, or pacing restrictions:
1. uncertain_delivery is never reopened automatically; stale sending becomes uncertain.
2. global send lease now has an owner and unique token; an old sender cannot release a newer lease.
3. one Telegram delivery attempt has a bounded runtime and becomes uncertain on timeout.
4. cross-account unified sending no longer copies another account's access_hash.
5. permanent privacy/blocked/deactivated failures are terminal in the unified pool.
6. active unified sends renew their owner lease with a heartbeat.
7. stale pending/reservation recovery respects a live lease and preserves uncertain guards.
8. retry_count now advances for genuine unified retry states.
9. tests were expanded for duplicate guards, lease ownership, hash isolation, stale recovery, and timeout.

Protected behavior not changed:
- @SpamBot automatic resume logic;
- first-DM phrases/templates and module selection;
- Kirill VIP funnel;
- existing user-configured delays and limits.

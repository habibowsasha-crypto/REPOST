TgBlaster v1.0.22b25 — fix: unified pool not sending
====================================================
BUG:
prepare_unified_send_for_account required either:
  - row in dm_unified_lead_accounts for this account, OR
  - non-empty target_username
"Ready" stats counted all due leads without that filter.
Result: UI showed hundreds ready + free slot, but send always got None
and released the lease → "Уже написали" stuck.

FIX:
Any due lead (pending/retry/unresolved, eligible_at<=now) is claimable
by any active account. Preference order:
  1) preferred_account = this account
  2) linked in lead_accounts
  3) everyone else
